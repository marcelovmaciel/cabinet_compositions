## CEBRI's Ordinary General Assembly Elects Board of Trustees for the 2025–2027 Term

  * Institutional
  * 29 october 2025

On Tuesday, November 28, CEBRI held its Ordinary General Assembly (OGA), during which it elected its Board of Trustees and approved the 2024 financial statements, audited by the independent consultancy PwC. Current Chairman of the Board of Trustees José Pio Borges was re-elected for the 2025–2027 term, along with Vice-Chairman José Alfredo Graça Lima and Luiz Ildefonso Simões Lopes.
