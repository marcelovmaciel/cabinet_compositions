### Paulo Jerônimo Bandeira de Mello Pedrosa's Biography

Mechanical engineer with 37 years of experience in government (executive and legislative), business, and academic sectors in the field of energy. He is the President of Abrace – Energia, an association representing large consumers. He is a member of the Board of Directors of Equatorial Energia, the Advisory Board of EPE (Energy Research Company), E+Painel, the National Industry Forum of CNI, and the Board of Directors of Equatorial Energia. He coordinates the Energy Working Group of Coinfra – Fiesp. He chaired Abraceel – the Brazilian Association of Energy Traders. He has served on the Boards of the National System Operator (ONS), Itaipu Binacional, and the distributors Light and Cemar. He was a Director at Aneel, Executive Secretary of the Ministry of Mines and Energy, and Interim Minister of State for Mines and Energy (MME).
