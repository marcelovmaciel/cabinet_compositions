# Final bounded historical snapshot follow-up

An openly accessible historical source was located: https://brasilia-df.filiaweb.com/ and its party lists. The site explicitly identifies its underlying TSE capture as 2016-07-27. It is an independent historical copy, not the current TSE API. The site provides name, masked voter number and affiliation date; it does not expose an adequate status/cancellation legend. Positive entries would therefore require careful identity and temporal adjudication.

All 35 Brasília party lists were scanned across 202 public pages, including all 26 PMDB pages. The parser inspected 182,698 rendered rows. No exact targeted officeholder name appeared, including Ivani dos Santos/ Ivany dos Santos and Carlos Henrique Menezes Sobral. Slight headline-versus-rendered-row discrepancies are preserved in HISTORICAL_SNAPSHOT_SUMMARY.json and are not repaired by inventing observations. Request logs retain page URL, date, response hash and row count; unrelated raw name lists were processed transiently and discarded.

The ordinary public name search is POST https://www.filiaweb.com/filiacaos/busca with field `busca`. It applies broad token matching and caps 50 results, making the global Ivani search incomplete; the full Brasília party-list scan avoids that cap for this locality. Carlos full-name query returns no results. Estella full/short legal names, Jonathas full name and Marcos Paulo full name return no results. Sergio José Pereira produces 12 exact-name city/party rows outside DF/Rio; these remain unverified possible homonyms, with no supporting identity link to the general. They are not affiliation evidence for him.

No new primary affiliation correction follows. Neither a current no-match nor an archived no-match proves non-affiliation. The previously documented Brasil.IO historical download remains behind account login; the old official ZIP 403 routes were not retried or bypassed. Perfil Político describes retaining history through 2020, but no directly usable scoped archival record was recovered during this bounded route. The completed Helder adjudication remains unchanged.
