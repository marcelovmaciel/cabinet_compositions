# Carlos Henrique Menezes Sobral: Alepi source follow-up

The original PDL 32/2021, process 27376/2021, was retrieved successfully from the [Alepi proposal PDF](https://sapl.al.pi.leg.br/media/sapl/public/materialegislativa/2021/17140/pdl-32.pdf). The four scanned pages contain the proposal and an attached Presidency/Secretaria de Governo CV. It was submitted in December 2021; the draft itself is dated December 12.

The CV lists Sobral's career in the Presidency, Health, Citizenship, Tourism and National Integration, parliamentary employment, councils and public honors. It does not state personal party affiliation or non-affiliation. The recurring MDB/PI page header identifies the proposing deputy, Henrique Pires; it must not be attributed to Sobral. The term Fundo Partidário in a listed expenditure-authorizing responsibility is likewise an administrative role, not a personal membership assertion.

Consequently this source does not establish his party on December 31, 2018. It does establish that the long public-service CV predates the 2023 appointment and can be retained as identity and career evidence. The attached honors in Cuiabá, Valparaíso and Luziânia are further biographical references, not membership facts.

## Independent interpretation of the 2023 Folhapress assertion

The retained Folhapress excerpt describes Sobral personally as a presumed MDB cadre and separately describes Geddel's sponsorship. This is stronger than a record of working for a party office. A primary MDB affiliation with a retained unaffiliated alternative is defensible as an explicitly uncertain journalistic-label adjudication for actual service after the January 2023 observation, provided the source is not represented as a formal membership certificate. The alternative captures the ordinary ambiguity of a party cadre who may lack formal affiliation. No other personal party is specifically supported by this source.

Use documented continuity forward only, with targeted contrary-event research. That 2023 description alone cannot be projected back to 2018. If independent MDB officeholders establish representation throughout the later service intervals, the personal ambiguity can be composition-neutral without declaring Sobral's individual affiliation certain.
