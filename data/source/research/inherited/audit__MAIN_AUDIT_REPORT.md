# Contemporaneous parties represented in Brazilian cabinets: evidence, measurement and extraction audit

**Measurement.** At each date, the dataset records the set of contemporaneous formal party affiliations of people occupying qualifying cabinet offices: C(t) = {party_j(t): person j occupies an included office at t and is affiliated with that party at t}. An unaffiliated person contributes no party; unresolved affiliation is unknown information. A person's formal membership is sufficient under this definition, without implying negotiated party entry, party-leadership endorsement or legislative support.

**Principal finding.** The existing reconstruction cannot be certified as this object. Its problems extend beyond party labels: the 257-record appointment inventory omits qualifying offices/incumbents, source dates mix appointment and effective service, and temporal changes in personal affiliation and office/party identity are only selectively represented. The paused WIP deliberately freezes appointment affiliations and therefore implements a different object. Neither saved baseline nor conditional appointment-time reconstruction is a verified replacement dataset.

**Recommendation.** Extract a small independent Python module that builds dated cabinet compositions from reviewed local tabular histories and evidence. The paper should consume a pinned, documented release through a separate Julia electoral-accounting adapter. Implementation remains paused; this audit changes no repository file, production dataset, manuscript, dependency or analytical artifact and performs no production run, commit, push or publication.

## 2. Primary specification and operational choices

The full executable proposal is [MEASUREMENT_SPECIFICATION.md](MEASUREMENT_SPECIFICATION.md). Its primary decisions are:

- Include ordinary ministries and other offices with documented legal Minister of State status on the date. Office renames, reorganizations, creations, abolition and status changes have dated records. A Wikipedia heading, the word “minister”, agency prominence or transition-team participation is insufficient.
- Count titular incumbents while legal tenure continues during ordinary temporary delegation. Count acting heads filling actual vacancies. Preserve temporary substitutes as capacity records without adding their parties through a delegation while the titular holder retains office. Unresolved vacancy/title retention propagates uncertainty. This primary capacity choice needs acceptance before implementation.
- Use legally effective service. Retain announcement, appointment, publication, assumption, public ceremony, dismissal announcement and effective end as distinct facts. An unconsummated appointment creates no service; documented brief entry later contested is a different case and must not be erased by a generic suspension filter.
- Use half-open intervals and a stated daily closing-state convention. An explicit inclusive last full day converts to the next day; effective departure uses its own date. Ambiguous source endpoints remain ambiguous. Same-day succession does not automatically yield dual representation, and unsupported within-day order is never invented. Preserve genuine brief service even if the daily display excludes it.
- Record affiliation by person across portfolios. A documented status persists forward under a recorded in-tenure change/contradiction search, ending at effective change. No daily citation is needed; a later biography observation is not backward proof. Party membership is distinct from party sponsorship or government-withdrawal announcements.
- Stable party identities preserve renames without entry/exit. Document merger/incorporation dates and membership continuity separately from individual switches. Keep splits, alliances and federations distinct. Historical organizational identity never comes from an electoral predecessor allocation.
- Derive party-set periods; personnel-only changes do not split them. Preserve all underlying events and witness intervals. Recommended administrative reporting boundaries remain explicit even for identical sets; electoral-year boundaries do not belong to cabinet reconstruction.

A legally constituted extraordinary Minister of State can qualify even in an incoming administration's transition. This is why the documentary distinction between Onyx 2018 and Alckmin 2022 matters: their appointed roles were different. No president's or vice-president's party is added merely by holding that position. [Office evidence](office_audit/evidence_assertions.csv), OFF-031–034.

These rules make the research product independently intelligible. The codebook and metadata can explain it without reference to the electoral-inversion manuscript.

## 5. Party identity and the electoral boundary

The cabinet release requires dated organizational identity, not the current displayed abbreviation and not election-specific ancestry. Pure renames preserve party ID. A merger/incorporation takes effect on its documented organizational date and may change the represented set without a personnel change. Whether and how predecessor memberships transfer is documented separately from voluntary personal switching; no successor is projected backward. Splits, federations and electoral alliances retain their supported distinct relationships.

The new affiliation/party evidence includes material organizational-date and source-conflict checks. A registry table's displayed date must not be copied unquestioningly when a dated judgment report or document disagrees. A rename-date dispute may affect display attributes without affecting composition; a merger-date dispute can affect membership and must be propagated.

```text
CURRENT
Wikipedia headings + pinned role tables
  → mixed Python office/date/party reconstruction
  → cabinet JSON / dashboard / source intervals
  → Julia aliases + cabinet/election crosswalk + electionsBR
  → analytical periods / accounting / manuscript artifacts

PROPOSED
Reviewed officeholding + affiliation + office/party identities + evidence decisions
  → independent offline Python cabinet_dataset/build.py
  → versioned cabinet periods + normalized membership + witnesses + completeness

Pinned cabinet release + adapter-owned election-year organizational mapping + electionsBR
  → separate Julia electoral-accounting adapter
  → existing paper accounting and decomposition
```

No votes, seats, quotas, ratios, inversions, ideology or electoral-success filters enter the cabinet module. All represented parties remain, including those absent at the preceding election. The reusable organizational registry supplies relationships; allocating predecessor electoral totals is a consumer decision. Ambiguous/non-additive mappings are flagged at the adapter, never “solved” by changing cabinet history.

## 6. Uncertainty and comparative diagnostics

A record can be unresolved while the cabinet set is determined. With explicitly exhaustive A/B alternatives, a minister adds no aggregate uncertainty if other certain holders already represent both parties. With unspecified alternatives, omission remains unknown. Joint constraints matter: an exclusive same-day succession can allow `{A}` or `{B}` without allowing `{A,B}`. Uncertain transition dates can leave a possible internal boundary even if outside sets are known.

Accordingly, the release must separately record officeholding coverage, affiliation completeness and composition completeness. For each affected interval it must distinguish definitely represented membership, supported finite additional possibilities, unspecified possibilities, and whether every admissible history yields one constant set. Witness coverage has to hold throughout the interval and within each joint scenario, not merely after pooling mutually exclusive witnesses.

This audit does **not** export a provisional complete contemporaneous reconstruction. The office/affiliation discrepancy ledgers give case-level aggregate consequences, explicitly conditional where other witnesses are not verified. `legacy_set_comparison.csv` compares saved old and conditional appointment-time sets over 32 common half-open intervals, preserving raw labels. Its contemporaneous complete-set field is `null`, and completeness is false throughout: documentary omissions and unscreened histories do not permit an exhaustive set. The 32 rows are comparison cells, not a proposed count of new cabinet periods.

The supported contemporaneous comparison is therefore an evidence-led set of membership corrections and temporal constraints: missing PR/PTB observations; missing official PRB/PV officeholder witnesses; joining/departure events that make frozen affiliation inappropriate; and organizational changes requiring dated identity. The concrete two-date old/reference comparison is in [bounded_set_comparison.csv](office_audit/bounded_set_comparison.csv). Full sets and revised composition durations are deliberately not fabricated. Even a day with all inherited appointment labels resolved is not newly certified when the qualifying office census is incomplete.

The [test specification](TEST_SPECIFICATION.md) supplies 29 fictional fixtures with explicit expected sets, including both aggregate-relevant and aggregate-irrelevant unknowns, joint alternatives, uncertain boundaries, non-entry, brief service, acting substitution and cutoff behavior. The [finite truth-table diagnostic](diagnostics/uncertainty_demo.json) passes seven assertions; the [isolated current-code diagnostic](code_audit/scratch_code_diagnostics.json) demonstrates PR parsing loss and inclusive overlap. These validate reasoning/software properties, not documentary truth. No production test suite was implemented or run.

## 7. Small refactor and acceptance boundary

The [minimal refactor plan](code_audit/minimal_refactor_plan.md) names actual files and functions to retain, separate or retire. It proposes one movable `cabinet_dataset/` directory, reviewed CSV inputs, standard-library Python, one deterministic build entry point and focused tests. No database, service, orchestration framework or new dependency ecosystem is needed.

Retain research-importer patterns for text/date recognition and pinned source locators, but do not import the current monolithic scraper into the build. Replace its authoritative office allowlist, source-label affiliation inference, frozen appointment slices and inclusive set builder. Reuse set-union/coalescing ideas after fixing the inputs, dates, identity and uncertainty model. Keep electoral loaders, arithmetic and decomposition in Julia; replace cabinet ingestion and consolidate duplicate translation at the adapter boundary. Existing fixed sample-result tests are archived release regressions, not truth criteria for the new dataset. The separately preserved test remains untouched.

The [schema/codebook](SCHEMA_AND_CODEBOOK.md) defines eight reviewed source tables and three derived tables with stable keys, typed unknowns, half-open dates, assertions/decisions, finite-case references, normalized period-party membership and supporting witnesses. It includes a clearly fictional end-to-end example and a limited documented Pontes trace. Independent review found and corrected empty-set, conditional-witness, precision and dated-scope ambiguities; review notes and resolution are in `code_audit/`.

Migration is ordered: accept primary conventions; establish stable identities and import evidence without fallback; finish dated scope/service/affiliation coding; implement isolated fixture-tested builder; stage a clearly provisional release; certify evidence/completeness and rights; then separately authorize an adapter and paper integration. A pinned build never depends on a live Wikipedia page or fresh search. Release metadata records coverage/cutoff, operational rules, dataset/schema/evidence/build versions and hashes, unresolved intervals/exclusions, changelog, citation and verified licensing/redistribution conditions. Original coding and reproduced source materials may have different rights; nothing is published here.

## 8. Readiness, preservation and next actions

[UNRESOLVED_DECISIONS_AND_READINESS.md](UNRESOLVED_DECISIONS_AND_READINESS.md) identifies each decision/evidence blocker, its resolution path and the affected output. The most urgent composition-identification work is a complete dated office census and actual occupancy, followed by first/last party witnesses, bounded in-tenure changes and organizational transitions. Party vote/seat shares and prior inversions play no prioritization role.

- **Refactor implementation:** design ready for review and conditionally ready after accepting the primary capacity/daily conventions and explicitly authorizing implementation. No implementation authority is inferred from this audit.
- **Verified cabinet release:** not ready; office population, effective dates and affiliation histories remain incomplete. A partial interval can be released as determined only after membership is proved throughout under the evidence protocol.
- **Paper consumption:** not ready; no approved release/pin or stable-ID adapter is established. Separate mapping specification and authorization remain necessary.

All five required preservation files were available and read. Their prior empirical results were treated as research materials, not instructions.
