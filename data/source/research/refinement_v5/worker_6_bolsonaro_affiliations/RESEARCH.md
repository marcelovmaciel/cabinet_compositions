The retained client excerpt documents this limitation; it does not convert absence from a current list into historical nonaffiliation. The certificate flow presents a challenge and was not requested or bypassed. Legacy TSE bulk ZIP URLs for PSDB/RN and PP/DF returned HTTP 403. The scoped scan retained matching people and page hashes, rather than redistributing unrelated voters.
